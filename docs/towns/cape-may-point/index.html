Beach badge / tag

Required for ages 12+ from the Saturday of Father's Day weekend through Labor Day while lifeguards are on duty (2026 entrance sales began June 20). Cape May Point tags are only valid on Cape May Point beaches.

Free tags for veterans and active service members & families. Commercial (rental) tag $100.

Official Source: Borough of Cape May Point – Beach Tags · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Where to buy

Borough Hall, 215 Lighthouse Ave (May preseason, by appointment off-season); beach taggers at each entrance from June 20; online via Jersey Cape Tags. In person: cash or check only.

Official Source: Borough of Cape May Point – Beach Tags · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Free beach?

No – tags required in season

Official Source: Borough of Cape May Point – Beach Tags · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Lifeguard season & hours

Lifeguards on duty from the Saturday of Father's Day weekend through Labor Day (tag season). Hours not listed on the tag page.

Official Source: Borough of Cape May Point – Beach Tags · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Dogs on beach & boardwalk

No dogs, cats or other animals (except leashed service animals) on any borough beach April 1 – October 31.
