Beach badge / tag

Required for ages 12+, Memorial Day – Labor Day

Seniors: $3.50 seasonal (preseason). Free veterans tags at City Hall. Ventnor badges are also accepted on Margate beaches (city welcome page).

Where to buy

Ventnor City Hall, 6201 Atlantic Ave (cash/check/card); online at jerseycapetags.com (mailed); My Beach Mobile app (digital); beach checkers.

Official Source: Ventnor City – Beach Information · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Free beach?

No – badges required in season

Official Source: Ventnor City – Beach Information · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Lifeguard season & hours

Guarded beaches between Memorial Day and Labor Day, 10:00 am – 6:00 pm daily (which beaches varies early/late season – call 609-823-7948).

Official Source: Ventnor City – Beach Information · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Dogs on beach & boardwalk

Beach: May 15 – Sept 30 leashed dogs only 7–9 pm on the high-tide-washed area; Oct 1 – May 15 leashed on the high-tide-washed area. Boardwalk: no dogs at any time.

Note: the codified ordinance text (Ch. 'Keeping of Animals') only lists the Oct 1 – May 15 allowance; the city beach page adds the summer 7–9 pm window.

Official Source: Ventnor City – Beach Information · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Alcohol rules

Alcoholic beverages are not permitted on the beach or Ventnor boardwalk during the summer months.

Official Source: Ventnor City – Welcome page · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Parking

Metered/kiosk parking exists near the beach (e.g., Suffolk & Somerset, Newport & New Haven Aves). Rates not published on the beach page.

Official Source: Ventnor City – Beach Information · Last checked Oct 17, 2026

Boardwalk / promenade

Yes – 1.7-mile residential boardwalk connected to the Atlantic City Boardwalk; summer bike hours (July 1 – Labor Day: weekends 6 am – noon, weekdays daylight); fishing pier at Cambridge Ave.

Official Source: Ventnor City – Welcome page · Last checked Oct 17, 2026
