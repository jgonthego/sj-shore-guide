About, methodology & sources

How this guide is built, what "verified" means, and where every fact comes from.

The town list

Ocean-beach municipalities come from the "Beaches" list in Wikipedia's Jersey Shore article (Ocean, Atlantic and Cape May county entries, consolidated to municipalities), plus Cape May Point. Bayfront and mainland towns (for example Linwood, Somers Point and Northfield) are added from each county's municipality list. Official website links come from each municipality's Wikipedia infobox and were then loaded to confirm they work.

What the labels mean

  • Fully verified 12 towns
  • Partly verified 5 towns
  • News-reported only 15 towns
  • Not yet verified 23 towns

Fully verified means beach badge rules, free-beach status, lifeguard season/hours, dog rules and alcohol rules were all confirmed on an official municipal page, municipal code (eCode360) or the official tourism authority. Parking and boardwalk details are verified where published. News report labels mark facts taken from news coverage (mainly Patch's 2026 badge guide) that we have not yet confirmed with the town. Anything else shows Not yet verified with a link to the official town site — we never guess.

Badge price year

All prices are for the 2026 summer season unless a page says otherwise. As of October 2026 most towns have not announced 2027 prices; Upper Township (Strathmere) has, because it starts charging for the first time in 2027.

Events

Events come only from official municipal calendars we can fetch automatically (WordPress "The Events Calendar" feeds, EvoGov and CivicPlus calendars) plus a small hand-checked list. Meetings, office closures and trash schedules are filtered out.

Disclaimer

This is an independent guide, not an official source. Rules, prices, hours and events change — sometimes mid-season. Always check with the municipality before you go. Swim only at guarded beaches while lifeguards are on duty. The "featured local business" box is a placeholder; no business is featured or endorsed, and no business has paid for placement.
